# Decision: Synthetic Navigator Only

**Date:** October 11, 2026
**Decided by:** Jose Luis Pacheco (entrant)

## The decision

The demo navigator is a **synthetic persona only**. The earlier plan to
ask a real Latinas Contra Cancer (LCC) navigator to run cases on camera
is dropped completely.

The persona is **Elena — Synthetic Demo Navigator / Navegadora sintética
de demostración**. She is labeled wherever she appears — in the UI, in
the approval record, and in the demo video:

> Synthetic / Sintética — not a real person / no es una persona real.

## What the submission MAY claim

- The full workflow was tested end to end with a simulated navigator:
  draft → barrier audit → resource verification → edit → approval.
- All patient cases in the demo are fictional and labeled as such.
- A real pilot with LCC navigators is **future work**.

## What the submission may NOT claim

- It may NOT say or imply that a real navigator tested the tool.
- It may NOT present Elena as a real person, a real LCC staff member,
  or a real testimonial.
- It may NOT claim real-world outcomes, times saved, or patient results.

## What does not change

Human-in-the-loop is unchanged for real use: in any real deployment, a
real human navigator/coordinator reviews, edits, verifies, and approves
every message before it reaches a patient. The synthetic persona exists
only inside the demo.
